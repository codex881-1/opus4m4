# The Pickup

A 70-second documentary short about the CIA's failed air pickup over Jilin, China, on the night of 29 November 1952. Two CIA officers, John Downey and Richard Fecteau, were shot down and held in China for 6,953 and 7,408 days. Washington called them "Army civilians" until President Nixon said, on 31 January 1973, "Downey involves a CIA agent."

**Deliverable:** `out/the_pickup_v1.mp4` (1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz, 69.9 s; stored with Git LFS).

Every frame and sound is generated by the code in this repo. There is no music: only narration, real recordings and a few synthesised accents.

## Structure

| Time | Scene | What it explains | How it's made |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Letter | The DCI's condolence letter, typed as the narrator reads the cover story | GLSL paper material (fibre height map, raking point lamp), per-glyph typewriter strike variation, macro camera tracking the strike point |
| 0:07 | Map | The cover-story route Korea→Japan is struck out; the real route goes K-16 → Jilin; crew cards; pickup zone at the DPAA crash-site coordinates | Real elevation relief (Lambert conformal), Natural Earth vectors clipped against the camera, 3D camera over a textured plane |
| 0:20 | Blueprint | How the All American pickup worked (15 ft poles about 50 ft apart, 50 ft cable, ~60 knots), the catch, the winch, then a rewind to show the trap: captured team, radio in Chinese hands, two guns in crossfire | Parametric C-47 built from published dimensions (NACA 2215→2206 wing), silhouette-edge line art, depth-occluded fill, engineering dimension lines |
| 0:31 | Ambush | Night reconstruction: moonlit valley, the lit zone, the second pass, white sheets flying off the guns, the crash in the trees | Real terrain at the DPAA datum (42°29′07″N 128°29′40″E), moon at 95% / alt 61° computed for that night, shadow-mapped moonlight, snow/rock/conifer shader, MSAA, additive tracers and fire |
| 0:46 | "You are Jack." | The words a Chinese officer said at capture | Typography with near-silence |
| 0:51 | Days | Every day in custody as a mark (Downey and Fecteau), key events, Washington's line struck at Nixon's quote, transcript card | Skia vector timeline under the single bulb that burned day and night |
| 1:00 | Lo Wu | The bridge into Hong Kong; the counter lands on **7,408 days**; title card | Layered parallax, mist, truss bridge |

Facts and sources are in `research/DOSSIER.md`. It covers claim-by-claim verification, a do-not-say list, and the CIA's own known errors. Prompts for optional photoreal inserts are in `docs/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.

## Pipeline

- **Picture** (`film/`): Python 3.12, `moderngl` (OpenGL 3.3 core, headless EGL; llvmpipe works) and `skia-python` for vectors and type. Scenes draw into HDR float buffers. A shared post chain (`engine/gl.py`) applies dual-filter bloom, halation, a filmic tone curve, split-tone grade, chromatic aberration, lens distortion, letterbox, two-octave grain and dither. `render.py` streams frames to FFV1, which ffmpeg then encodes to x264.
- **Edit lock** (`film/scenes/timeline.py`): scene cuts and animation beats are computed from the narration's word timestamps (`audio/vo/timing.json`), so picture follows voice to the word.
- **Voice** (`audio/vo/`): 7 voices (Kokoro ×2, Pocket TTS ×5) × 12 lines were scored for naturalness (UTMOS) and accuracy (Whisper WER) in `scores/`. The `jane` voice won and got 3 more seeds per line. `assemble.py` takes the best take per line, applies a light broadcast EQ, places lines on the grid and extracts word timings.
- **Sound** (`audio/design/mix.py`): 22 CC0 field and foley recordings plus earshot accents, placed from word timestamps. It uses convolution reverbs (room, concrete, outdoor), voice-driven ducking and level rides under key lines, and masters to -15 LUFS integrated with true peak about -1.5 dBTP. See `audio/assets/CREDITS.md`.

## Rebuild

```bash
apt-get install -y libegl1 libgl1-mesa-dri fonts-noto-cjk ffmpeg
uv venv && uv pip install numpy scipy skia-python moderngl glcontext pillow soundfile pyloudnorm shapely pyproj
bash film/tools/fetch_assets.sh            # fonts + Natural Earth
python audio/assets/fetch_recordings.py    # CC0 recordings
# narration (separate venvs): pocket_take.py -> score.py -> assemble.py  (writes narration.wav + timing.json)
python audio/design/mix.py                 # -> audio/mix/soundtrack.wav
python film/render.py --out out/render/full.mkv
ffmpeg -i out/render/full.mkv -i audio/mix/soundtrack.wav -map 0:v -map 1:a -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 18 \
  -tune film -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 320k -movflags +faststart -t 69.9 out/the_pickup_v1.mp4
```

Preview individual frames with `python film/render.py --only blueprint --frames 24,90 --png /tmp/frames`. A full render takes about 45 minutes on 2 CPU cores with llvmpipe.
